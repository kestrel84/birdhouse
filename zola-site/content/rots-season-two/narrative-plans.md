This season takes place in the same universe as season one and features the same player characters. 

See [the season one brainstorming docs](@/riders-on-the-storm/brainstorming.md) for details more around the outside of the blacksphere and loose details about the Something Empire and Peraxian Federation.

## Worldbuilding
### Peraxian Federation
The vibes here are a fusion of two eras of USA - 1800s wild-west expansionism (think cowboys, bureaus, railroads, ocean liners) and 1950s-60s cold war (think CIA, vast and shadowy government ops, paranoia and scaremongering).

### Something Empire
The vibes here are a fusion of cold war era Soviet Union and the Imperium of Man. Think enormous temples, autocracy and oligarchy. This is more of a WIP than the Federation.


## Narrative
The players uncover the story as written through notes, environmental storytelling and other means I have not determined.

The plan for the narrative is kind of integrated with the [structure](@/rots-season-two/gameplay-plans.md#structure), in that the players choose where to go based on the information they have and the leads they wish to pursue.

There are three narrative threads:
- Graves the super spy
- Shuri the researcher
- Calliope the undercover agent
