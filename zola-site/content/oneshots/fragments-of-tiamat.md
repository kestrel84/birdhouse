## World
At the center of the world stands Auris, a great and holy city, center of the planes and dominion of the Church of the Seven Comets.
None who are judged unworthy may enter, and sinful and forbidden magic will be punished with death.

It is surrounded by great industry and prosperous agriculture out many miles, but farther out, the sky cracks.
Many aeons ago, a devil-worshipping magician made a pact with Tiamat, the world-eater, and great calamity followed. Now all planes are as one.
Further from the protection of the holy city, the elemental planes begin to creep in, soon followed by Heaven and Hell, before all is absorbed into the endless fog of the Astral. 
The plane of air is to the north, earth to the east, fire to the south and water to the west.

There are some who dare venture into the remmnants of the Fracture. Those who brave the plane of fire are flamewalkers, those who conquer the plane of air are skyfarers, those who wander the plane of earth are nomads, and those who plunder the plane of water are divers.

## Blacktooth Rock
This is where we start our story. A lonely town hanging precariously off the edge of a cliff before the endless seas, it is host to many a diver and more than a little vice and sin.
Places this far out are some of the last refuges of the warlocks and sorcerers, those hunted by the church, and if you're lucky you might even catch a wandering druid or bard.

It is so named for the foreboding sight, far off in the waves, of a pitch-black rock shaped like a sharp tooth. None dare venture near.
Well, almost none. Someone with too much incense and not enough candles has hired a couple of crews to retrieve an artefact rumoured to contain secrets from before the Fracture.
