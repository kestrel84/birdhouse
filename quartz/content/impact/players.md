# Rough summary of the players and their stories so far
## Thalanir Cross (Eoin)
Aka Tal.

A half-elf bladesinger whose parents are a princess of Lorrokan and a human. He's not part of the royal lineage as the family never acknowledged his mother's relationship.

His staff's a fragment of the mystical Starfall Oak, an ancient tree once struck by a meteor somewhere on the edge of Lorrokan. His sword's from a homeless guy he brought a drink for. It's a [sword of lifestealing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5390-sword-of-life-stealing).

He's in Geronimo and the west generally in search of answers and a journey to Laravin's Hope. Known by reputation to most of the town as one of a few local wizards.

## Rick Lockjaw (Trish)
The sheriff of Geronimo.

Was at one point a normal human, working as the sheriff. He got ambushed and killed by bandits while protecting a shipment of something or other. He then appeared before the Arbiter, who offered him his body in exchange for getting to drive one day a month. His phylactery is a vial of something; this also powers his gun.

He's a gunslinger/fighter type.

## Gorm the Gobbler (Sam)

A dwarven cleric of someone or other and a certified gremlin. Has a pointy hat and the spirit of a gnome.

## Marek (Kavi)
Comes from some tiny village on the edge of the desert. An outcast and delinquent because of his goliath size, he awakend his sorcerous powers after injuring someone by accident. Exiled from his home, he walks the desert in search of that which is hidden, looking for an expedition to Laravin's Hope.
