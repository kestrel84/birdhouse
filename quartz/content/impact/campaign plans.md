# Plans for the upcoming impact campaign

Already established plot elements:

- Villain: The Arbiter, a vaguely hell-associated entity powerful enough to grant warlock pacts
- Place: Laravin's Hope, legendary 'shining city' far in the west.
- Event: the fall of Laravin's hope into fire and destruction, as seen in a vision by Thalandir
- Geronimo and all that's happened in it
- The rest of the world, most notably Lorrokan and its politics; a chunk of senior members of the monarchy and state have just gotten murked
  - Idea: Perrin and Alice get word of an approaching elven military unit and tell the party (or specifically, Thal and Miro) to get the hell out of doge since they have connections to the monarchy.
    This kicks off the campaign.

Plan is to run ten sessions or so; real time I have until the end of placement in september to run this, and no house in bath for the last two weeks of august.
If I start a week on wednesday at time of writing (that is, 20/05) then I'll have 10-12 sessions.
I'll plan ten sessions under the assumption that stuff will run over and my plans will generally get messed up.

## Structure

### Act 1

Should be three sessions or so.

Objective: find and enter Laravin's Hope.
The plan is that the city is hidden as a mirage, always getting further and further away, requiring some solution,
and then once they can approach it, they have to find a way past all the wards, protections and physical barriers it has around it.
The act finished with them arriving in the city itself.

Sessions:

1. The PCs arrive at the city and, along with some new NPCs, discover that it's impossible to get near. They begin the search for a solution 
(at present, a magical macguffin in some ancient tomb somewhere, subject to change). Has combat in the back half.
2. After some more combat, the PCs retrieve the macguffin and get to the edge of the city, which has a number of more normal magical and physical defences (traps and the like). They begin to get through them.
3. The PCs finish traversing the edge of the city and arrive in Laravin's Hope. See [[#Laravin's Hope]].


### Act 2

Should be four sessions or so.

Objective: find the source and meaning of the visions.
By this time a number of other characters will have appeared with similar rings and visions. They also recur every now and then, getting worse over time.
This eventually leads to a confrontation with the Arbiter, who reveals that this whole convoluted plan was to lure a bunch of powerful adventurers to Laravin's Hope and use them for some nefarious scheme.
The Arbiter's plan broadly succeds and the players are put on the back foot before act three.

Sessions: 

1. The PCs dick around in Laravin's Hope, generally looking for a mystic, oracle, or diviner of some kind in order to help them understand the visions.
This diviner tells them that the source of the visions is hell, and they need to go there to find out more. Furthermore, there is some permanent link to hell deep in the city's underworks.
2. The PCs go and find the gates of hell, and there fight a big demon who tells them that the Arbiter's after the city for some reason.
3. ???
4. The PCs confront the Arbiter, have a fight, and are basically defeated before getting deus ex machina'd to a safe location.

### Act 3

Should be three sessions or so.

Objective: stop the Arbiter, but for real.
The Arbiter has retreated to some dramatic location, like the top of a tower of some kind, and hell portals are popping up all over the place, generally wreaking havok and rendering the visions true.
The players have to get to the location and defeat him.

Sessions:

All three are to be one giant setpiece; the Arbiter's holed up at the highest point of the city, a giant, impossible, dracula's castle type skyscraper.

1. Find a way into the skyscraper.
2. Climb it.
3. Defeat the Arbiter, for real this time. 

## Laravin's Hope

Details about the city itself.

It's _extremely_ magical, much much more than normal, and has a bunch of spelljammer-ass exotic races around. A lot of stuff just floats, everything is lit with magic, and anything else I can think of.
