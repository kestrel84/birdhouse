# 1: Come up with a concept
Who do you want your character to be? This can be a name, a class, or even just some vibes. Some examples:
- A wandering swordsman, made sharp in blade and mind by the harsh outer reaches of civilisation.
- An heir to a throne, trained from birth in sorcery to rule over their kingdom.
- A street urchin who got by in the big city with a silver tounge and a poisoned dagger.
- An outlander chieftan, moved to action after a threat to their village.
- A magic school droput with a half-filled spellbook and a penchant for destruction.
