# The sequel to the previous impact oneshot
## Overview
Ol' Hieronymus is up to something with the gold shipment that the PCs delivered last time. It's been a few months since the last oneshot and, the _Theridiae_ is back in town with another shipment, and some passengers from Lorrokan. He's going to hold a party in his old-ass rickety mansion, but the _Theridiae's_ captain feels like something's off. Getting absolutely rotten vibes from the passengers.
 
The passengers are all old elven royalty, known to Thalandir (eoin) but no-one else this far west. Not the top of the top, more uncles and cousins looking for a leg up and something impressive to show the queen.  They're here at the request of Hieronymus, who purports to be able to show them Laravin's Hope. In fact, the devil (perhaps the Arbiter?)  who told him this was lying and it'll just open a hell portal, which is bad.

This oneshot has three phases:
1. Get into the gathering (disguise, sneak, bust down the doors, anything else)
2. Discover and accesss the secret basement of the mansion
3. Stop the ritual (combat)
