## Session 8
The PCs have gotten themselves out of the hole they were in after getting blasted by the Arbiter,
with the help of Lancelot, an elven cleric of Lathandar with wings, and Theo, the guy with long hair and a buster sword.

They now need to ascend the spire of stars, a three-piece skyscraper, and bonk the Arbiter.

Theo and Lancelot don't join the PCs, as they need to stop the demons popping up all over the city. Cedric goes with Lancelot to try and
regain his faith or something, Ria goes with the party.

This session will be the first of the three sections of the skyscraper.
Each one has something special; The second one the PCs will be able to rotate it.

As for the first one, it's a little something like this.

While the PCs are briefing in the guard post with Theo and Lancelot, they get a map of the tower. But when they go outside,
a huge storm has kicked up around the whole tower. The entire thing is wrapped in a level-apropriate version of a [dragonstorm](https://2e.aonprd.com/Hazards.aspx?ID=88) (just half the damage and -15 the saves).
This isn't a huuge problem except when you're around windows, which is unfortunately most of the tower.

There are three encounters in the first section of the tower; the first and third are fairly normal, consisting of two [nabasu](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=600) and one [erinye](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=111).
The second is three [sarglagons](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2908);
this fight takes place in a section of the tower shaped like a massive spiral staircase with a 10ft diameter column of water rising through the middle.
All three fights take place in arenas dotted with windows, creating areas where the storm is constantly blasting, changing effects every round.

Note: if the PCs want to climb a nearby building and geronimo it over to the spire of stars, they can. This will skip half of the second floor (when I make it lmao),
but is made trickier by the fact that the second floor is in a storm. Should the PCs choose to take a nearby building, it has a magic elevator in it, but it gets jammed to full
by some demons (erinye and buddies from earlier, 2 waves of those three) who also try and ambush them. The PCs are then on a 30 second (5 round) timer to kill all the demons so they don't get pasted
on the ceiling of the lift. They then need to figure out how to a. find a window through the storm and b. actually jump the ~60ft gap. Imma leave that one to the players.

At the top is a vault of magic items (no runes or weapons, though) and a teleporter to the second floor.



note for later: the Arbiter has a transformation into an [adult mocking dragon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=4151)

The second floor has the fight against the sarglagons, with the added ability to rotate the building.
Roughly speaking, the segment is a hexagonal prism, with a column of water down the middle.
The water is suspended magically, and there are still windows letting the storm in.
