## Session 4
As of the end of last session, the PCs have just arrived in the under-undercity and seen Cedric get his ass beat by a demon (specifically, a [coarti](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2907)).

So they fight the demon, rescue Cedric, and if they loose, get rescued by Theodore (who is reportedly currently hunting demons).
During the fight, the demon mentions that he's in servitude to a very powerful demon lord who's got nefarious plans.
The arena is composed of a bunch of platforms floating on lava, which occasionally sink or rise from the lava. There are nine platforms in total, in a square, and five on the surface at any given time.
Two sink and two rise per round.
After the fight, assuming they haven't just gotten rescued, they meet Theo back in the guard post and get clearance to go up to the next level (level three, I think).

> [!NOTE] For now, there are five layers.

Theo also has a new apprentice badass, a youngish, very tall and well-proportioned redheaded tiefling. A bit of a femme fatale, she's called Ria Vasari; gameplay class swashbuckler.
(Given the opportunity, she does sleep with tal, but nicks his money and drugs in the process).
More importantly, she's also new to the city and has a prophecy ring. She and Theo both suggest going into the undercity (not the hell bits though) to find a shady diviner/oracle character and determine the source of the wishes.
Cedric declines to come with them but is cagey as to why (on the off chance the players inquire, persuasion is required. The actual reason is cause he's more focused on finding Laravin).

As for finding the oracle, there are a few avenues. Nearby the guard post are three taverns and a covered market. The taverns are the Refinery, the Hawk's Nest, and the Rusty Nail.

They have no luck in the Refinery, some in the Nest, and a lot in the Rusty Nail. This far up, it's also the only entrance into the undercity.

### The oracle
The oracle, one Xoravek K'sul, is a xixchil with *many* eyes. In addition to the two primary eyes, there are two rows of tiny eyes below each large one, appearing as points of light.
He expects payment - he initially pitches 700 gold, but can be negotiated down pretty easily, taking 4-500 without too much resistance. He'll also take favours or errands.
The errand he has ready is to go and plant some false memory emitters to mess up a rival oracle. Would be a cakewalk, but they need to not be seen by the rival while doing it, and she's got a load of scrying eyes around her area.

Upon being paid/the errand completed, he takes the two rings, and reveals that the vision is indeed legitimate, and is a time soon to be (the worst kind of vision).
The source of the vision is deep in the undercity; near the grave of a legendary warrior (Percival Laravin, but they don't know that yet). Laravin made a pact with the Arbiter, a very long time ago,
hiding it from his fellow knights. It was a pretty small pact, as they go, simply a magic sword, but it revealed Laravin's Hope to the Arbiter. Despite the fact that the city soon planeshifted,
the Arbiter began planning for its eventual return, planting the rings around promising adventurers likely to make it to the city.

One Arbiter monologue later, they fight! I'll try and put together something unique for the fight. The PCs have to loose, before getting deus ex machina'd by Theo as the Arbiter swoops up to the spire of stars.
