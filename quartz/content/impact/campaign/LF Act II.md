### The oracle
The oracle, one Xoravek K'sul, is a xixchil with *many* eyes. In addition to the two primary eyes, there are two rows of tiny eyes below each large one, appearing as points of light.
He's still getting his mojo back after a rival oracle (who he simply refers to as 'old hag') planted a bunch of false memory emmitters around his hideout, causing all his divinations to go haywire.
He wants the PCs to go on an errand both as payment for his services and as a way to buy time.
The errand he has ready is to go and plant some false memory emitters to mess up a rival oracle. Would be a cakewalk, but they need to not be seen by the rival while doing it, and she's got a load of scrying eyes around her area.

Upon being paid/the errand completed, he takes the two rings, and reveals that the vision is indeed legitimate, and is a time soon to be (the worst kind of vision).
The source of the vision is deep in the undercity; near the grave of a legendary warrior (Percival Laravin, but they don't know that yet). Laravin made a pact with the Arbiter, a very long time ago,
hiding it from his fellow knights. It was a pretty small pact, as they go, simply a magic sword, but it revealed Laravin's Hope to the Arbiter. Despite the fact that the city soon planeshifted,
the Arbiter began planning for its eventual return, planting the rings around promising adventurers likely to make it to the city.

One Arbiter monologue later, they fight! I'll try and put together something unique for the fight. The PCs have to loose, before getting deus ex machina'd by Theo as the Arbiter swoops up to the spire of stars.


### The stealth section
The PCs have to plant three magic macguffins in three specific spots. Each macguffin must be charged up by recalling memories, the more the better. So the players better get their stories ready!

In each of the three places, there are a number of scrying eyes, and other hazards.
- The first place has a number of dudes standing around, in addition to a couple of scrying eyes. The dudes are a bunch of dwarves Ria stole from a while back, they'll really not be pleased to see her.
If she's not there for some reason, the PCs still need to explain what they're doing to the dwarves and why they're sneaking around.
- The second place has a whole bunch of laser tripmines. You can't see them, except for the first couple, which are illuminated by a leaking steam pipe.
- The third area is a rather larger cavern with a lot of pits and other normal-ish hazards, as well as some hidden side passages.

If the whole stealth mission thing doesn't take very long, then the PCs need to get up to level five, as Percival's grave is below level five, almost directly in the center of the city.
They have two basic options - go through the normal undercity, contending with a budding turf war in another underground market and stopping Ria nicking shit every two seconds, or go through the under-undercity, which involves fighting some demons
(in this case, four [wrecker demons](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=4318) and a [hellbound attorney](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=4326) ordering them around).


As for stats for the new weapons:
- Rick's fancy pistol: A +1 striking pistol inscribed with a custom rune: grants the ability to charge the pistol with an energy damage type, granting it +1d8 damage of said type, in addition to a special effect. The available types are:
  - Acid: inflicts Sickened 1.
  - Cold: inflicts Enfeebled 1.
  - Electricity: inflicts Stunned 1, but only on the enemy's critical fail.
  - Fire: inflicts 1d8 persistent fire damage.
  - Sonic: inflicts Deafened.
- Thal's six pistols duct-taped together: More or less what it sounds like. You can fire the pistols individually, though they are unupgraded duelling pistols, dealing only 1d6 damage.
You can also elect to fire all six at once, which grants it kickback (-2 to atk unless you have +2 strength or more) and forces a misfire roll, but causes it to now deal 2d20 damage.

As for the arbiter himself, he appears as an dude, basically, in an immaculate coat and tails, with a staff. He floats a good foot of the ground, and has the basic stat block of a [coloxus](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3363).

He can't die and may deal more damage if the fight goes on too long.

The giant arthropods in question are [ogre spiders](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=4562).
