# Laravin's Folly Act I

See [[campaign-plans#Act 1|Act 1 plans]] for a brief summary.

## Session 1

Opens with Gavin and the *Theridiae* showing up again, with a message for the party in general and Thaladin and Miro in specific: there's an elven military detatchment en route from Volundir, and they'd better not be here when it arrives.

He offers to take them onwards, as he's already got a commission from an existing passenger to take them out to the last known oasis, the Pyxis Oasis.
This passenger is Cedric, a lone knight of Lathandar who used up all his savings to by passage in the hope of picking up Laravin's trail. He tells the party about Laravin's past as a knight of Lathandar a hundred or so years ago.

Assuming they take him up, they end up out at the oasis. They can see Laravin's hope very faintly in the distance - it looks like a glowing turqouise/aquamarine skyline with an exceptionally tall central building.
Any attempt to reach it lands them back at the Oasis. This requires a crystal ball of planar seeing to get through.

Said crystal ball is inside a long-buried temple, with walls covered in Heiroglyphs and no lighting whatsoever. It was put there by Laravin when the temple wasn't buried, and guarded by Gaheris, one of his party.
Getting to the ball requires first entering the temple, down some stairs which have fallen into the abyss, leaving a ~100ft long chasm in its place. There are a few places on the wall which are standable but they need to be seen through the dark.

After that, they need to get past the lock incongruously overlaid on the big obvious main door. This lock requires three medallion pieces, which form a complete medallion of the knights of Lathandar.
They are each in a trial room, and each have a different trial.

- Trial of wit: A puzzle or riddle of some kind.
  - This is going to be something like the following: The players are given a ring of twelve tuning forks (matching the circle of fifths) and a bunch of chord names ($C$, $B\flat m$, $Eb^{ 13 }$ )
- Trial of might: A fight.
- Trial of agility: Light six torches in twelve seconds in a room with no floor, but strung up with a ton of ropes.

The corpse of Gaheris is puppeted by the Arbiter when they try and get the ball. Gaheris is placed in the middle of the temple, in center stage of an amphitheater-type room.
There are three statues of long-dead gods in the room, which can be knocked over; there are also quicksand puddles around the place to get stuck in.

### Enemies
For the trial of might, three level 5 enemies:
- x2 [living statues](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3997) 
- x1 [divine warden of nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=1121)
  - This is reflavoured as a divine warden of Lathander, summoned by Laravin when he was here. It wears armour strikingly similar to Cedric's.
- The fight takes place in a more or less boring arena, but with a large (7-hex) pillar in the middle.

For the final fight, a level seven enemy and two level 3 enemies.
- Gaheris himself is a bound knight of Lathandar, willingly serving into undeath. His stat block is that of a [Deathless Heirophant](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=1854).
- The two lackeys are summoned by Gaheris on his first turn; they are long-dead servants of Lathandar, drawn back into temporary service. They are [Deathless Acolytes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=1853).
