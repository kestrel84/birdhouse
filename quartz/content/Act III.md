### Session XVI (16/04/2026)

Wha Happen:

- The party collected their reward for clearing out the asteroid, then immediately used almost all of it up travelling to the elves' location, a planet called New Sylvia. This took a month or so and everyone trained up.
- Upon arrival at the elven homeworld, they are greeted by Eivor, a friendly young space dragon (normal dragon shape but the same size as the storm chaser and their body looks like [this](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/img/champion/splash/AurelionSol_0.jpg))
- He informs them that his partner, Rose, is planetside talking with an old friend, and offers to lead them to the city (though he's a bit big to land there himself)
- The storm chaser passes by a super star destroyer-sized abandoned ship in orbit of the planet, and it appears to be made of remnant crystal
- The party land in the elves' city, and discover that it's a mishmash of landed, converted ships and a few new buildings, some floating on the many, many rivers and canals criscrossing the whole city, some landed on solid ground.
- Eivor has informed Rose of the party's arrival and they are greeted by her and her friend, an elderly-looking elf in a military uniform with lots of badges. She's called Hermia and she immedately invites the party to a military outpost of some kind to discuss Plot Stuff™. Turns out the rumours about one of the alliance's planets getting swallowed are true. The elven navy's bureaucracy is running too slowly and Hermia needs the party's help to get around them and get something done.
- Said something is to raid the abandoned ship in orbit for a unique material which can be fitted to the storm chaser to let it survive inside the blacksphere by acting as a powerful conduit for weave energy.
- Rose starts trying to resurrect Graves, but runs into some hitches and can't finish it in time for the raid.
- The raid begins with securing the chosen docking location (notably the ship is in zero gravity).
- To be continued!

### Session XVIII

So what went down last session is basically as planned up to extracting the macguffinite from the core and getting it down to New Sylvia; then we stopped on Hermia taking the party to an armoury/vault in order to gear up for the next session, basically. Also level nine.

General plans for the session:

- The PCs travel via hell to get to Xiaojin posthaste - it takes a few hours instead of a month (corollary: you could cross the galaxy in like a few days and the blacksphere would be pretty tiny by comparison)
- The party blows up the ship Darius is on in order to aid the evacuation, which kicks off the fight with Darius proper in the ruins of the ship
  - This is facilitated by the fact that the PCs have already done this (though not on an operational ship)
- Phase one of the Darius fight
- Darius then jumpstarts the blacksphere, forcefully pulling Xiaojin into the sphere.
- Phase two: They fight Darius inside the blacksphere.

Now this boss fight is gonna need some _real_ sauce in order for it to live up to expectations. Some ideas:

- Darius is constantly shit-talking
- He's not just constantly throwing around two high-level spells a turn
- I want this to feel like a good raid boss, so some mechanics would be ideal.
  - For the sake of time, this'll have to be fairly simple. Make it dead obvious in phase one and switch it up in phase two.
  - Perhaps reuse the zero-g mechanics; they're pretty fun. Force engagement by (for instance) having four switches which all need to be thrown in different corners of the arena.
    - they can't really be destructible crystals or anything else you can just snipe; this would provide no challenge
    - or perhaps destructible crystals which have a shield around them, crown of sorrow crystal style
    -
  - If the fight needs to be in zero g, then perhaps make it in the wreckage of Darius's ship?
    - perhaps single-use pieces of terrain? i.e., you can stop/push off once only.
    - actual gravity fluctuations
- A health bar would be cool
- Darius slowly becomes more and more corrupted over the course of the fight
  - perhaps phase two is 'remnant darius' and he's more or less completely consumed?

How the PCs fight:

- CB: battle master, uses a spear. Has fancy moves. Exclusively physical and almost exclusively piercing damage.
- Eeta: ancestral barbarian, also uses polearms. Tanky. Exclusively physical and almost exclusively piercing damage.
- Graves: rougue of some kind. Prefers ranged, has a couple of pistols and a hand crossbow. Also almost exclusively piercing damage. Also has a bunch of tricks and random bullshit.
- Azeglio: artificer. Has a cannon, by default deals piercing damage but can also deal a good amount of radiant and force. Some random spells n shit going on as well.
- Olem: gunslinger. Crit sniper. Deals extremely high damage if he hits a crit. Can deal piercing, fire, lightining, or thunder damage depending on which rune he uses.

All in all, _heavy_ weighting towards piercing. Normal amount of damage is ~10-20 per turn, and ~30-40 if they're critting (perhaps more for olem.)
